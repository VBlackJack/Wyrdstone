# Changelog

All notable changes to Wyrdstone are documented in this file.

## [Unreleased]

- **Web (WASM32 / WebGL2) & GitHub Pages**: Added full `wasm32-unknown-unknown` browser support (`web/index.html`, responsive PC and smartphone canvas with safe-area padding and touch-scroll lock, browser `localStorage` primary/backup saves via `SaveData`, `js_sys::Date` wall-clock regen, WebAudio gesture unlock, `.github/workflows/pages.yml`, and CI `check-web` job).
- **True Endgame Loop (Cyclic Skald Pass, Endless Decrees, Yggdrasil Wonder & Nine Realms Ascension)**: Added infinite **Cyclic Skald's Path** resets (+15% reward scaling per cycle up to +150% with preserved overflow XP), infinite **Endless Jarl's Decrees** after Saga quests (bonus tool every 5th decree), the **10-stage Yggdrasil World-Tree Wonder** monument on the Fjord map (+harvest & Boss damage bonuses), and **Nine Realms Ascension** (Midgard -> Jotunheim -> Nidavellir -> Vanaheim -> Asgard with distinct realm atmospheres, scaled enemies/rewards, and preserved permanent unlocks).
- **Building Mastery 5-Star, Repeatable Collections, Valkyrie's Aid & Quick-Claim Bar**: Extended building progression to 5-Star Mastery with passive perks, made Realm Collections repeatedly chargeable for Glory and resources, added **Valkyrie's Aid** defeat recovery tiers, and added a one-tap **Quick-Claim** Fjord reward strip.
- **Adaptive PC + Android UX/UI & Input Polish**: Added responsive viewport layouts (`Portrait`, `Compact`, and `DesktopWide` dual side-rail Match-3 layout dedicating ~94% of vertical height to the board), Android `SafeAreaInsets` + `HapticEvent` hooks, 5-tab portrait Fjord bottom navigation bar, desktop in-place hover tooltips (`Tab`, `Q`, `E`, `G`, `F1`, `F`), animated Fjord harvest fly-to-HUD orbs, live two-booster fusion blast footprint and Super-Combo previews during swipe drag, and a Skald's Path XP progress bar on the Victory screen.
- **Retention Loop & Guided 90s FTUE**: Added 4-tier **Valhalla Flame** win streaks, 1x/run **Last Stand** defeat revive (preserving live board and streak), **Offline Harvest Vault** (8h-14h scaled by Great Hall tier, >=30 min absence), **Daily Bounties** (3 UTC contracts), free 10-tier **Skald's Path** reward track, guided fast first-board FTUE coaching, and progressive **PlayMode** milestone unlocks.
- **Endgame Depth (Patron Devotion, Weekly Challenge & Legend Ranks)**: Added a 5-tier **Patron Devotion Altar** per deity (+Fury speed, +score, Tier 3 starting Fury, Tier 5 Boss damage), deterministic **Weekly Seeded Challenges** with Bronze/Silver/Gold/Legend medals, and **Legend Ranks** beyond High King (+2% spoils per 250 Glory, capped at +40%).
- **Yggdrasil Trials & Ragnarok Gauntlet**: Added **6 zero-RNG Yggdrasil Trials** (`assets/data/trials.ron`) with tight move budgets, Par Seals, free entry, and instant 1-click Retry; added the 4-Boss **Ragnarok Gauntlet** rush facing Fenrir, Jormungandr, Surtr, and Hel consecutively with shared moves (+8 per stage) and carried-over Divine Fury and Ragnarok Overdrive.
- **Dual-Slot Amulets, Divine Synergies & War Draughts**: Reaching Great Hall Tier 2 unlocks a 2nd Amulet Band slot (`equipped_amulet_secondary`); pairing matching Patrons or two Tier-3 Amulets activates 5 **Divine Synergies**; the Runic Forge brews 3 single-raid **War Draughts** (Mead of Poetry, Valkyrie Elixir, Surtr's Brew).
- **Drakkar Expedition 2.0**: Upgraded roguelite runs with **6 collectible Expedition Relics**, **3 risk-reward Skald's Oaths**, 6 rotating obstacle board layouts, and **Wave-5 Boss encounters**.
- **Valhalla Blitz (75s Arcade Mode)**: Added a fast-paced timed score-attack mode with infinite moves, a x1-x8 Momentum Multiplier, +2s combo/booster time extensions, Divine Fury timer freezes, Bronze/Silver/Gold/Valkyrie medals, and Blitz records in Chronicles.
- **Tactical Combat Depth**: Added Patron **Favored Runes** (+50% Fury gain), **Ragnarok Overdrive** (3 turns of 2x score, +50% boss damage, and frost spread immunity), and **Boss Stagger & Exposed Wards** interrupting imminent ripostes.
- **UX Overhaul**: Streamlined hub header into logical groups with claimable notification badges, redesigned Building Inspector around a hero banner + compact difficulty selector, added live Match-3 star pips, 50% Boss Phase-2 HP threshold ticks, Pause Restart shortcut, touch tool AoE previews, late input buffering, inline Mead refills on Defeat, and booster-combo hint prioritization.
- **4th Mythological Boss (Hel), Daily Fjord Blessings & Collection Crests**: Added **Hel, Queen of Helheim** (`boss_hel`, Fog of War + `CurseRunes` riposte + painted portrait), deterministic UTC **Daily Fjord Blessings** (Odin's Favor, Njord's Breeze, Fenrir's Eclipse, Idunn's Grace) shown in Fjord City and applied to every board, and 5 painted Realm Collection emblems in the Collections modal.

- **Data-Driven Gameplay Tuning**: Login tribute table, daily streak scaling and tools, win-streak boosters, clan rank perks, building bonuses and upgrade perks, amulet tier steps, mastery ladder, star thresholds, daily blessings, patron fury values, Daily Trial generation, Endless Expedition pacing and boons, and boss damage/phase/curse tuning now live in `balance.ron` and the content RON files; achievements declare their unlock condition in `achievements.ron`. ### Changed
- **Mobile Play Area, UX & Contrast Polish**: Maximized Match-3 board size in both Portrait (`4px` side margins, compact mobile frame/HUD clearances) and PhoneLandscape fullscreen (board-first dynamic side rails from `122-210px`, scrollable right rail, compact top-bar mode below `760px` width, wide-phone `< 500px` tall landscape classification, Portrait Fjord aspect preservation without image stretching, orientation-aware Android safe areas in `PreUpdate`, `visualViewport` + `:fullscreen` web sync, full 44px Title utility hit targets, 1-tap City drawer switching above the scrim, and WCAG AA contrast on Title Fullscreen/Windowed buttons).
- **Dependabot & Dependencies**: Grouped weekly Dependabot updates by ecosystem (Cargo and GitHub Actions) with ignore rules for pinned `bevy`/`bevy_winit` (`=0.15.2`) and `rand >= 0.9.0`; updated `ron` to `0.12`, `directories` to `6.0`, and GitHub Actions workflow pins (`actions/checkout` v7, `actions/upload-artifact` v7, `actions/download-artifact` v8, `softprops/action-gh-release` v3).
- **Single Turn Engine**: Match-3 turn resolution (swaps, tools, Divine Fury, cascades, relic drops, end-of-turn hooks, victory boosters) now runs through one step machine in `wyrdstone_core`; the animation layer only plays its steps. A differential fuzz against the previous behaviour proves the move changed no rule. Clear order is now deterministic.

### Fixed
- **Fjord Map Buildings**: Buildings fill their sprite box again and stay anchored to their terrain spot on every window size; rebuilt buildings show their tier stars and the chapter dialogue opens after the rise animation.
- **Board Clearance**: More space is reserved under the Match-3 board so the tool bar no longer overlaps it.
- **Unwinnable Content**: `meadery_1` now targets a rune that actually spawns; Expedition wave 1 only targets runes inside its pool; Daily Trial relics never land on blocked cells. Data-feasibility tests cover every bundled level, a full year of dailies, and Expedition waves.
- **Board Dead-Ends**: The shuffle fallback is verified, swaps must form a match involving a swapped cell (hints agree), and unshufflable boards are regenerated instead of soft-locking.
- **Save Safety**: An unreadable save is never overwritten: it is copied aside, the `.bak` is tried, and the session stays read-only (with a persistent warning) while the original could still load. Saves are fsynced and a rotating `.bak` is kept.
- **Newer-Version Saves**: A save written by a newer Wyrdstone is never treated as corrupt nor overwritten: the session is read-only with a dedicated warning, and an explicit New Saga or Import first preserves the newer files (`.newer-*`, `.bak-newer-*`) and aborts if that copy fails. Loaded saves are sanitized (tiers, stars, streaks, energy clamped; resource arithmetic saturates).
- **Login Tribute**: Rolling the clock back can no longer re-claim the daily tribute.
- **Match-3 Polish**: Stale hint glows reset, Undo restores the stats of the undone move, the countdown pauses during cascades, mute refreshes the HUD, HUD texts update in place during cascades, and embers/overlays follow window resizes.
- **UI Polish**: Scrollbars only appear when content overflows, BGM volume shows real percentages, Continue is offered only when real progress exists, Reduced Motion covers city build animations, and button enabled state is explicit.
- **Android Platform**: Save path from the app's internal data dir, BGM paused and progress saved on suspend, Back button mapped to Escape (City -> Title -> quit), packaging switched to NativeActivity with full `configChanges`, signed release APK via repository secrets, and CI now builds the Android APK.
- **Endurance Regen**: Driven by the wall clock, so time spent in the background or asleep is credited.
- **Touch & UI**: Buttons fire on release so touch scrolling never buys or picks anything; notices show as a toast on the hub and Title; portrait pins no longer overlap; every chapter speaker has a portrait; Undo state refreshes; HUD clicks no longer reach the board; text on accent buttons meets 4.5:1 contrast; tappable controls are at least 44 px; composite UI strings come from locale templates; the Windows build no longer opens a console.
- **Fog of War**: Prevented invisible matches inside fog, fog-targeted Bifrost Prisms, hidden starting/Fury boosters, and shuffle deadlocks on fogged tiles.
- **Free Tools & Divine Fury**: Stopped free tools and Patron Fury from ticking Boss Riposte counters or triggering Niflheim Frost spread.
- **Eye of Odin Amulet (Tiers 1-3)**: Scaled starting boosters from 1 to 3 across upgrade tiers and updated the Amulets modal to show live scaled effects.
- **Boss & Daily Rewards**: Applied reward multipliers (Freya's Fury, Amulets, Longship Dock, Clan Rank, remaining moves) to Boss Raids and displayed streak-scaled rewards on Daily Trial victory.
- **UI & State Polish**: Cleared `active_expedition` on Retreat and Expedition Boons close/Escape, timed Boss portrait hit-flash reset, prevented nested Harvest buttons from triggering parent pin selection, synced global `M` mute shortcut with Title/City labels, removed unused `city.panel.building.cost` locale key, and avoided full board rebuilds on invalid adjacent clicks.
